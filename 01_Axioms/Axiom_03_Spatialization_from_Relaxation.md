# Axiom 3 — Spatialization from Relaxation

> **Structural Causal Chain:**
> Constraint → Differential Tension (ΔΦ) → Quantized Step (δs) → Cadence → Metric Emergence (g_μν) → Observable Dynamics

**Purpose:**
Derive spatial extent as the historical record of structural relaxation.

**Role in Causal Architecture:**
- **Stage:** Foundational Axiom
- **Function:** Axiom 3: space emerges as the record of structural relaxation.
  Spatial extent accumulates through quantized relaxation steps:

  $$\delta s = s_0 \cdot n, \qquad n \in \mathbb{Z}^+$$

  so that any measurable distance $s$ is an integer-weighted multiple of the
  elementary step $s_0$. The accumulated path determines observable phase:

  $$1+z_{\text{obs}} = (1+z_{\text{metric}})(1+\delta z_{\text{foam}}), \qquad
    \delta z_{\text{foam}} = \int_0^s \frac{\nabla|Z_{\text{medium}}|}{Z_0}\,ds$$

  — i.e., the expression is a proposed mapping between relaxation history and a candidate
  redshift contribution; its empirical validity remains to be tested.
- **Upstream:** Axiom 2 (constraint priority)
- **Downstream:** Axiom 4 (cadence quantization), S operator
