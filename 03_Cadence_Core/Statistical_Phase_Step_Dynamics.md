# Statistical Stability of Dynamic Phase Steps
### پویایی گام‌های فازی و پایداری آماری قانون برآمده

## 1. Motivation

A dynamic substrate requires local variation. If every admissible step were exactly identical and every constraint configuration were invariant, the model would reduce to a static or perfectly periodic repetition with no mechanism for evolving structure.

The proposed distinction is therefore:

$$
\text{local fluctuation}\neq0\quad\text{while}\quad\text{macroscopic statistical drift may approach zero}.
$$

## 2. Step Variables

For step \(n\), write

$$
\Delta\Phi_n=\overline{\Delta\Phi}+\epsilon_{\Phi,n},
$$

$$
\Delta s_n=\overline{\Delta s}+\epsilon_{s,n},
$$

$$
\Delta\tau_n=\overline{\Delta\tau}+\epsilon_{\tau,n}.
$$

The individual residuals need not vanish.

## 3. Statistical Closure

A candidate closure condition is

$$
\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=1}^{N}\epsilon_{x,n}\rightarrow0\qquad(N\rightarrow\infty),
$$

for the relevant aggregate variable \(x\), subject to explicit assumptions on bias and correlations. A stronger probabilistic statement, such as convergence in probability or almost surely, must not be assumed without specifying the stochastic model.

The important conceptual point is:

$$
\boxed{\epsilon_{x,n}\neq0\;\text{locally}\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad\text{law unstable macroscopically}.}
$$

## 4. Why the Fluctuation Is Mechanistically Necessary

The fluctuation changes the admissible transition from one step to the next:

$$
S_n\xrightarrow{\Delta\Phi_n,\;\Delta s_n,\;\Delta\tau_n}S_{n+1}.
$$

If all relevant step variables and constraints were invariant, then

$$
S_n\rightarrow S_{n+1}\rightarrow S_{n+2}\rightarrow\cdots
$$

would contain no endogenous mechanism for changing the transition conditions. Thus local variability is proposed as a necessary ingredient of the dynamic picture. This is a structural hypothesis, not yet a mathematical necessity theorem.

## 5. Aggregate Propagation

For a path containing \(N\) steps, define

$$
S_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\Delta s_n,\qquad
\tau_N=\sum_{n=1}^{N}\Delta\tau_n.
$$

Then an effective path speed is

$$
v_N=\frac{S_N}{\tau_N}.
$$

The observer accesses the aggregate ratio. The individual microscopic residuals can remain unresolved.

The causal condition is kept separate:

$$
v_N\le c\quad\text{in the regime where the model identifies the path speed with an effective causal propagation speed}.
$$

## 6. Relation to the Three Candidate Outputs

The same local fluctuation principle may be carried into the three open quantities:

$$
B_{{\rm spec},n}=\overline B_{\rm spec}+\delta B_n,
$$

$$
\Delta\Phi_{q,n}=\overline{\Delta\Phi_q}+\delta\Phi_{q,n},
$$

$$
C_{{\rm sub},n}=\overline C_{\rm sub}+\delta C_n.
$$

Their means or coarse-grained limits may be stable even when their local realizations fluctuate. Whether this is actually true must be derived from the constitutive model.

## 7. Dynamic Law vs. Measurement Error

The residuals \(\epsilon_n\) are not automatically measurement errors. They represent candidate physical/local variation in the mechanism. Measurement uncertainty is a separate quantity and must not be conflated with the model's internal fluctuations.

Thus:

$$
\boxed{\text{physical step fluctuation}\neq\text{observer measurement error}.}
$$

## 8. Required Mathematical Closure

To promote this principle beyond a qualitative hypothesis, the project needs:

1. a definition of the probability or ensemble structure, if stochasticity is intended;
2. a deterministic alternative if fluctuations are generated by chaotic or nonlinear dynamics;
3. a proof or explicit sufficient condition for aggregate convergence;
4. a constitutive relation linking constraint alignment to step residuals;
5. a demonstration that the resulting macroscopic law is not inserted by hand.

Until these are supplied, the correct status is **dynamic hypothesis with a proposed statistical stabilization mechanism**.
