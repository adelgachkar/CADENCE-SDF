# Master Causal Chain (SDF)

## 1. Foundational Ordering

The proposed mechanism distinguishes a prior state from a posterior state:

\[
S_{\rm pre}\neq S_{\rm post}.
\]

A transition therefore has an orientation,

\[
S_n\xrightarrow{T_{\mathcal C}}S_{n+1},
\]

and the boundary/constraint determines the admissible transition set.

\[
\boxed{
\text{Distinction}
\rightarrow
\text{Boundary}
\rightarrow
\text{Directionality}
\rightarrow
\text{Quantized Transition}
\rightarrow
\text{Cadence}
\rightarrow
\text{Relational/Metric Response}
\rightarrow
\text{Observable}
}
\]

This is the **proposed mechanism**, not an empirical proof of the ontology.

## 2. Mapping to the Existing Seven Stages

The existing seven-stage notation is retained:

\[
\mathbf{G\to C_{id}\to S\to R\to M\to L\to F}.
\]

The mechanistic interpretation is:

1. **G — Ground State:** reference structural state for admissible configurations.
2. **Cid — Structural Constraint:** boundary and constraint conditions that delimit allowed transitions.
3. **S — Spatialization:** accumulated record of admissible structural steps.
4. **R — Relational Geometry:** connectivity and boundary-conditioned relations.
5. **M — Metric Emergence:** candidate effective metric representation.
6. **L — Lattice/Structural Dynamics:** stress, relaxation and state transitions.
7. **F — Flux & Field:** observable propagation and field-level outputs.

## 3. Directionality

Directionality is not inserted merely as an arrow in a diagram. It is represented by an ordered transition operator and an oriented increment:

\[
T_{\mathcal C}:S_n\rightarrow S_{n+1},
\qquad
\Delta s_n,\Delta\Phi_n.
\]

Irreversibility is **not assumed automatically**. If an arrow of time is to be derived, the model must specify a non-invertible transition rule, dissipative term, boundary condition, coarse-graining, or other mathematical source of asymmetry.

## 4. Boundary and Cavity

A cavity/void is represented as a region with modified structural and transport conditions. The zero-boundary idealization refers to vanishing active-loss response,

\[
R_{\rm loss}\to0,
\]

while reactive response may remain finite.

Therefore:

\[
\text{cavity}\neq\text{nothing},
\qquad
\partial\Omega_{\rm cavity}=\text{active constraint}.
\]

## 5. Observable Projection

The proposed transition from structural dynamics to observables is:

\[
(\mathcal C,\Delta\Phi,Z_{\rm medium})
\rightarrow
\delta s_n
\rightarrow
\omega_c
\rightarrow
\Delta\Phi_{\rm opt}
\rightarrow
\Pi_C
\rightarrow
\mathcal O.
\]

Candidate observables include expansion-like response, optical redshift, rotational response and effective propagation.

## 6. Gravity and Environmental Crossover (Proposed Extension)

For the gravity channel, the current hypothesis is:

$$
M\rightarrow\mathcal C_M\rightarrow\mathcal F_B(\mathcal U_{\rm foam})\rightarrow J_{\rho,\rm eff}\rightarrow g_M.
$$

The observable response may contain an environmental component,

$$
g_{\rm obs}(r)=g_M(r)+g_{\rm env}(r),
$$

so that a near-source effective proportionality can cross over toward an environmental/noise floor at large distance. A horizon is treated as a possible strong boundary condition on the same band-limited mechanism. These are proposed mechanisms, not empirical conclusions.
